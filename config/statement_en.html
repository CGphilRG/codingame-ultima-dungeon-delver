The Goal

Solo Optimization puzzle inspired by Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar. Guide the Avatar through a perilous dungeon, cast Britannian runic spells (FLAM_POR and MANI), collect as much Gold and Virtue Relics as possible, and extract via the Exit Stairs before your torchlight burns out!

 Rules

The dungeon is a 13×11 grid of walkable stone tiles (.) and impassable walls (#). The Avatar starts with:

  • HP (Hit Points): 40 / 40
  • MP (Mana Points): 20
  • Torchlight: 65 turns (decreases by 1 at the start of every turn)
  • Gold: 0 (your optimization score criterion)

Dungeon Entities

  • C — Treasure Chest (25 Gold, 0 HP): Collected automatically when the Avatar steps onto its tile.
  • R — Virtue Relic (60 Gold, 15 Guardian HP): Guarded by an ancient spirit (entityHp = 15). While entityHp > 0, it deals 4 damage per turn if adjacent (Manhattan distance ≤ 1). Once its HP is reduced to 0, stepping onto its tile claims the Relic for +60 Gold.
  • M — Dungeon Monster (20 Gold, 15 HP): Deals 4 damage per turn when at Manhattan distance ≤ 1 from the Avatar. Slaying a Monster immediately awards +20 Gold.
  • F — Healing Fountain (0 Gold, 0 HP): Stepping onto a Fountain restores +18 HP (up to 40) and replenishes +10 Torchlight turns.
  • E — Exit Stairs (50 Gold + remaining Torchlight bonus): Stepping onto the Exit Stairs immediately completes the dungeon run and awards +50 Gold plus +1 Gold per remaining Torchlight turn!

Avatar Actions & Runic Spells

Each turn, the Avatar moves 1 step orthogonal along the shortest walkable path toward (x, y) via MOVE x y, and may optionally perform one combat or magic action:

  • STRIKE tx ty (0 MP, range 1): Melee strike dealing 8 damage to the Monster or Relic Guardian at (tx, ty).
  • FLAM_POR tx ty (5 MP, range ≤ 3): Britannian fireball spell dealing 15 damage to the Monster or Relic Guardian at (tx, ty) (instantly slaying a full-HP Monster or Guardian).
  • MANI (4 MP, self): Britannian healing spell restoring +14 HP (up to 40).

Viewer Debug Mode

Use the RENDER MODE toggle in the viewer settings menu to switch between RPG SPRITES (2D RPG sprites with dynamic Torchlight halo) and GEOMETRIC DEBUG (color-coded geometric shapes with entity type codes).

Victory Conditions
  • Reach the Exit Stairs (E) before running out of HP, Torchlight, or turns, while maximizing your total Gold collected.
Defeat Conditions
  • Your hp drops to 0 or your torch reaches 0.
  • You fail to reach the Exit Stairs within 70 turns or your program times out.

 Game Input

Initialization Input

Line 1: 2 space-separated integers width and height, the width (13) and height (11) of the dungeon grid.

Input for One Game Turn

Line 1: 6 space-separated integers avatarX, avatarY, hp, mp, torch and gold, the Avatar's current coordinates, Hit Points, Mana Points, remaining Torchlight turns, and collected Gold.

Next height lines: a string row of length width representing one row of the dungeon (. for floor, # for wall).

Next line: an integer entityCount, the number of active entities remaining in the dungeon.

Next entityCount lines: 6 space-separated values entityId, type, x, y, value and entityHp:

  • entityId: unique integer ID of the entity.
  • type: character code (C = Chest, R = Virtue Relic, M = Monster, F = Fountain, E = Exit Stairs).
  • x, y: integer coordinates of the entity.
  • value: integer Gold value awarded upon collecting or slaying this entity.
  • entityHp: remaining integer HP of the Monster or Relic Guardian (0 if unguarded).
Output for One Game Turn

A single line containing a movement command optionally followed by an action:

  • MOVE x y
  • MOVE x y STRIKE tx ty
  • MOVE x y FLAM_POR tx ty
  • MOVE x y MANI

Example: MOVE 6 5 FLAM_POR 7 5

Constraints
width = 13, height = 11
1 ≤ entityCount ≤ 15
Response time for the first turn ≤ 1000 ms
Response time per turn ≤ 50 ms
Maximum number of turns: 70
